Our Role

A delivery partner that stands behind its work

The Filler Piece was established to address a clear gap in the joinery industry — the absence of a delivery partner that takes genuine ownership of outcomes from project commencement through to final sign-off.

We exist to protect the brands and commercial interests of joinery manufacturers, builders, and developers. Our focus is on delivering measurable outcomes — conforming installations, independent quality assurance, targeted remediation, and specialist artisan works — under a single accountable structure.

What sets TFP apart is not just what we do, but how we do it. Every engagement is governed by documented quality and safety frameworks, with clear scope, defined deliverables, and evidence-based sign-off at every stage.

Our Delivery Philosophy
TFP is built around a simple principle: outcomes matter more than activity. We are commercially accountable for the results we are engaged to deliver — from the first site visit through to documented sign-off.

Every project engagement is supported by structured quality frameworks, clear scope, and disciplined documentation — giving manufacturers, builders, and developers the confidence that comes from working with a partner who stands behind their work.
Core Principles

What we stand for

ACCOUNTABILITY
Full responsibility for the outcomes we are engaged to deliver. One partner, one scope, one standard of sign-off.
SYSTEMS OVER EGO
Quality outcomes result from documented systems, not individual effort. Assurance is built into how we work, not just what we produce.
COMPLIANCE AS STANDARD
WHS obligations, contractor governance, and documentation are integrated into every project from engagement through close-out.
BRAND PROTECTION
Joinery is the final expression of a manufacturer's or builder's quality. We treat protection of your brand as a core deliverable.
COMMERCIAL RIGOUR
Clarity of scope, defined deliverables, and documented outcomes in every engagement. No ambiguity. No scope creep.
INDEPENDENT PERSPECTIVE
Our QA function operates with genuine independence. We report what we find — because honest assessment protects everyone in the chain.

Service 01

JOINERY
INSTALLATION

End-to-end managed installation coordination. One point of accountability from delivery to sign-off.

What We Do

Managed installation,
without the risk

TFP manages the complete joinery installation process on behalf of manufacturers and builders — coordinating site interfaces and ensuring work is executed in conformance with drawings, specifications, and the project programme.

Scope Includes
  • End-to-end coordination of joinery installation across all project types
  • Site interface management with builders, head contractors, and project stakeholders

Engaged when manufacturers or builders require a managed delivery partner to coordinate and oversee joinery installation — particularly where brand exposure or quality requirements make an unmanaged approach commercially unacceptable.

Joinery manufacturers protecting their product post-production. Builders and developers requiring a compliant, accountable installation partner. Projects where standard installer engagement does not provide sufficient governance.

A fully managed installation programme, delivered on programme, in conformance with specification, and supported by documentation that provides genuine evidence of quality. One partner accountable. Zero ambiguity.

Service 02

QA
AUDITING

Independent quality audits — evidence-based, commercially structured, built around your sign-off requirements.

What We Do

Independent QA,
without compromise

TFP conducts independent quality audits of joinery installation during the programme or following completion — structured around your drawings, scope of works, tolerances, and project requirements, delivering evidence-based reporting that provides genuine compliance assurance.

Scope Includes
  • Independent quality audits during installation or at project completion
  • Defect identification documented to drawing and specification reference
  • Compliance reporting structured for project and contractual requirements
  • Alignment verification against drawings, scopes, tolerances, and standards
  • Evidence-based sign-off documentation suitable for practical completion
  • Structured defect schedules for follow-up remediation if required
When It's Used

Engaged when an independent quality assessment is required — at mid-project hold points, prior to practical completion, following installation by others, or as a standalone verification service on complex or high-value projects.

Who It's For

Builders seeking independent sign-off prior to handover. Manufacturers requiring confirmation their product has been installed to specification. Architects and PMs requiring documented evidence that installation meets design intent.

Client Outcome
A complete, evidence-based quality audit report. Defects identified, documented, and referenced against specification. Confidence in the state of the works before sign-off is given — not after.

Service 03

JOINERY
REMEDIATION

Targeted rectification — protecting timelines and reducing rework cost without unnecessary disruption.

What We Do

Rectification without
unnecessary disruption

TFP manages remediation of non-conforming joinery works, from minor defect correction through to complex rectification programmes. Our approach prioritises targeted intervention — resolving non-conformances without full removal wherever structurally and commercially achievable.

Scope Includes
  • Assessment and scoping of non-conforming joinery works
  • Targeted rectification without full removal where achievable
  • Complex remediation programme management for multi-defect projects
  • Coordination of specialist remediation resources where required
  • Post-remediation quality verification and sign-off
  • Documentation of rectification scope and outcome for contractual purposes
When It's Used

Engaged when joinery works have been identified as non-conforming — through independent audit, builder inspection, or client feedback — and a structured rectification programme is required to restore compliance.

Who It's For

Builders facing defect liability exposure. Manufacturers responding to non-conformance claims. Developers managing rework cost on complex residential or commercial projects.

Client Outcome
Non-conforming works rectified to specification. Timelines protected. Cost minimised through targeted, managed remediation. Full documentary evidence provided at close-out.

The TFP Model

How managed delivery actually works

The Filler Piece operates as a managed delivery partner that takes on full commercial responsibility for defined project outcomes — governed through structured quality, safety, and documentation frameworks.

Every project engagement follows a consistent process — ensuring governance, accountability, and documentation are embedded at every stage from scoping through to formal close-out.

What This Means for You
You deal with one partner. One point of contact. One party accountable for delivery, quality, safety, and documentation. The complexity of site coordination and quality assurance is managed within TFP's framework — not transferred back to you.
Engagement Process
Step 01
Initial Enquiry & Project Scoping
We begin every engagement with a structured scoping conversation — understanding your project requirements, programme constraints, quality expectations, and site-specific considerations before providing a formal proposal. No assumptions. No generic quoting.
Step 02
Proposal & Engagement Agreement
A clear, documented proposal covering scope of works, deliverables, quality framework, WHS obligations, and commercial terms. The engagement agreement defines what TFP is responsible for — in plain, commercially sound language.
Step 03
Pre-Mobilisation Planning
Prior to site commencement, we conduct a structured pre-mobilisation review — confirming site readiness, reviewing drawings and specifications, and establishing the quality and documentation framework for the project. Nothing goes to site without a plan.
Step 04
Managed Delivery
TFP manages delivery with site oversight, program adherence, and in-progress quality reviews — with regular reporting that keeps you informed without burdening you with operational detail.
Step 05
Quality Review & Sign-Off
At completion — or at defined project hold points — TFP conducts a formal quality review against the agreed scope and standard. Defects are documented, addressed, and re-verified before sign-off is provided. No rubber-stamping.
Step 06
Close-Out & Documentation
Full documentation of works performed, quality outcomes, and any rectification undertaken. A complete project record — suitable for contractual, warranty, and practical completion purposes.

1.0 Purpose and Intent

This Privacy Policy establishes the framework by which The Filler Piece Pty Ltd collects, holds, uses, and discloses personal information in the course of its business operations. The intent is to ensure all personal information is managed lawfully, transparently, and with respect for the privacy rights of individuals, in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles and all applicable privacy legislation.

2.0 Statutory Compliance

This Policy is issued in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). It supports compliance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). The Filler Piece complies with the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) Scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Where a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, TFP will contain and assess the breach, notify affected individuals, and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

3.0 Definitions

The Filler Piece / TFP means The Filler Piece Pty Ltd including its business operations. Personal Information has the meaning given in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Client means any builder, joiner, developer, or other party who engages TFP to perform or manage works. Website means thefillerpiece.com.

4.0 Scope and Application

This Policy applies to all personal information collected, held, used, and disclosed by The Filler Piece Pty Ltd regardless of the medium in which it is held. It applies to all personnel, service providers, clients, builders, and visitors to the Website who submit enquiries or contact forms.

5.0 Personal Information We Collect

TFP collects: contact and identity information (names, addresses, email, phone); licensing and compliance information (ABN, licence numbers, insurance details); financial information (bank account details, payment information); engagement records; work health and safety records; site photography and video; and website visitor information submitted through contact forms. TFP does not collect sensitive information as defined under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) as part of its ordinary business operations.

6.0 How We Collect Personal Information

TFP collects personal information directly from individuals wherever reasonably practicable — through company forms, contracts, and induction documents; direct communication including email and telephone; submission of enquiries through the Website; provision of credentials by service providers; and site-based activities including photography and evidence records.

7.0 Why We Collect Personal Information

TFP collects personal information to manage engagement relationships; verify licensing, insurance, and compliance credentials; process invoices, payments, and financial transactions; manage project activities, scope documentation, and quality assurance; meet WHS obligations; communicate with clients and business contacts; respond to Website enquiries; and comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

8.0 Storage and Security

TFP takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, and disclosure through controlled access permissions, password protection, multi-factor authentication, encrypted transmission, and restricted physical access. Personal information will be retained for the minimum period required by law and legitimate business need, then securely destroyed or de-identified. Some third-party platforms used by TFP (including Microsoft 365) may store data on servers located outside Australia. TFP takes reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards apply.

9.0 Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The most current version will be published on the Website. Continued use of the Website constitutes acknowledgement of the current version of this Policy.

10.0 Disclosure to Third Parties

TFP does not sell, trade, or rent personal information to any third party for commercial purposes. TFP may disclose personal information to accountants, insurers, technology and platform providers, regulatory authorities, and legal advisers where necessary for business operations or required by law.

11.0 Website, Cookies and Analytics

The Website may collect personal information submitted through contact forms. The Website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools (including Google Analytics) to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable data. Visitors may configure their browser to refuse cookies. The Website is not directed at children under 18.

12.0 Direct Marketing

TFP may use contact information to send communications about its services where the individual has provided their details in connection with a business relationship. All electronic marketing will comply with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). Individuals may opt out at any time by contacting info@thefillerpiece.com.

13.0 Access and Correction

Individuals have the right to request access to personal information TFP holds about them and to request correction of inaccurate information. Requests must be submitted in writing to info@thefillerpiece.com. TFP will respond within a reasonable time in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

14.0 Complaints and Enquiries

Any person who believes TFP has breached its privacy obligations may submit a complaint in writing to info@thefillerpiece.com. TFP will acknowledge receipt within a reasonable time and provide a written response within 30 days. Where a complaint cannot be resolved, the individual may escalate to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
